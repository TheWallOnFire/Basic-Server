# 08. Frameworks & Compliance

Security isn't just about hacking; it's about following established standards and managing risk.

## 1. Industry Frameworks
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
- **MITRE ATT&CK**: A globally-accessible knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques.
- **OWASP Top 10**: The standard awareness document for web application security.

## 2. Compliance
- **SOC2**: Service Organization Control.
- **HIPAA**: Health insurance portability (USA).
- **GDPR**: Data protection (EU).
- **PCI-DSS**: Credit card data security.

## 3. Risk Management
- **Risk Assessment**: Identifying vulnerabilities and threats.
- **Risk Mitigation**: Reducing the impact or likelihood of a risk.
