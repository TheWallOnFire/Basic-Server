# 09. Multimodal AI

AI is no longer just about text. Modern models can see, hear, and create in various formats.

## 1. Vision (Images)
- **Understanding**: Describing images, extracting text (OCR), identifying objects.
- **Generation**: DALL-E 3, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion.

## 2. Audio & Speech
- **STT (Speech-to-Text)**: OpenAI Whisper, AssemblyAI.
- **TTS (Text-to-Speech)**: ElevenLabs, OpenAI TTS.
- **Real-time Voice**: GPT-4o voice mode, Gemini Live.

## 3. Video
- **Understanding**: Analyzing video content and time-stamps.
- **Generation**: OpenAI Sora, Kling, Luma Dream Machine.
