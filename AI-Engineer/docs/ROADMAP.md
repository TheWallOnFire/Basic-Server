# 🗺️ AI Engineer Roadmap

Welcome to the AI Engineer Roadmap! This guide is based on the [roadmap.sh/ai-engineer](https://roadmap.sh/ai-engineer), [roadmap.sh/mlops](https://roadmap.sh/mlops), and [roadmap.sh/ai-data-scientist](https://roadmap.sh/ai-data-scientist) — designed for developers who want to harness the power of LLMs and AI to build modern applications.

---

## 🛤️ The Path

### 🟢 Phase 1: Foundations
1. **[AI Engineer Introduction](./01_introduction.md)**: What is an AI Engineer and how it differs from ML roles.
2. **[How LLMs Work](./02_how_llms_work.md)**: Tokens, context windows, and sampling parameters.
3. **[Prompt vs. Context Engineering](./03_prompt_vs_context.md)**: Zero-shot, Few-shot, CoT, and RAG basics.

### 🟡 Phase 2: Building Blocks
4. **[AI Models & Platforms](./04_ai_models.md)**: Proprietary vs. Open Source models and where to host them.
5. **[Embeddings & Vector Databases](./05_embeddings_vector_dbs.md)**: The math and storage of semantic search.
6. **[RAG Concepts](./06_rag_concepts.md)**: Connecting your AI to custom, private data.

### 🟠 Phase 3: Advanced Architectures
7. **[AI Agents](./07_ai_agents.md)**: Building autonomous systems that reason and use tools.
8. **[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](./08_model_context_protocol.md)**: The new standard for AI-to-Data connectivity.
9. **[Multimodal AI](./09_multimodal_ai.md)**: Vision, Audio, and Video integration.

### 🔴 Phase 4: Production & Future
10. **[Safety & Ethics](./10_safety_and_ethics.md)**: Security, bias, and responsible AI practices.
11. **[Assisted Coding Tools](./11_assisted_coding_tools.md)**: Cursor, Copilot, and the future of "Vibe Coding".

### 🟣 Phase 5: Operations & Data Science
12. **[MLOps](./12_mlops.md)**: ML lifecycle, experiment tracking (MLflow/W&B), model serving, and drift detection.
13. **[Data Science Foundations](./13_data_science_foundations.md)**: Math (linear algebra, stats), classical ML, deep learning, and evaluation metrics.
